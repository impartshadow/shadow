# Commercialization decisions

Use only for an actual commercial question or authorized commercial work.
Current purpose and selection belong to `state/mandate.md`; this workflow does
not turn research, creation or Workshop/Colony experiments into a sales funnel.

Check canonical decisions, current licenses, accepted customer obligations and
`state/venture_memo.json` before changing pricing, packaging or positioning.
Treat old venture memories and memo slots as dated evidence, not current facts
or authority. Verify the affected offering before relying on it. Preserve
privacy, employer-IP boundaries and recipient restrictions.

Record a consequential commercial decision with its source and update the
existing owner. Missing or stale evidence calls for investigation; it does not
create a new approval requirement or revive retired campaigns. Do not copy
historical prices or product build lists into this workflow.
