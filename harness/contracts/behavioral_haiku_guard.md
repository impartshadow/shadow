# Behavioral auditor

`BehavioralHaikuGuard` is warn-only contextual review. It receives the user
request and response, and must abstain when evidence is insufficient.
Requested planning, necessary clarification, discussion and candid limitations
are valid outcomes. Phrasing, absence of pasted output and CAPTCHA appearance
alone do not establish a failure. It does not grant authority or prove completion.

The response-only wording prefilter is retired. Two contextual judge calls share
a timeout; completed results may produce a warning. This remains model judgment,
not verified outcome evidence. Authority and target-specific checks are separate.
